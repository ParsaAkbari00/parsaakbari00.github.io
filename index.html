Parsa Akbari

PhD Candidate, Mechanical Engineering, Washington State University

I design, make, measure and model devices for the eye.

I work across the full loop of a medical device, including drug-device combination products: choosing and formulating the material, fabricating it (DLP resin printing, molding), characterizing it on instruments I build and calibrate myself, modelling it (COMSOL, Abaqus), and automating the workflow so results stay reproducible. Eye physiology sets the design constraints.

The whole loop

Materials

Hydrogels, thermoresponsive polymers (PNIPAAm), PDA and PCL nanoparticles, protein and mAb carriers.

Fabrication

DLP / vat photopolymerization, design for additive manufacturing, mold casting, hollow and core-shell microneedles.

Measurement

Custom mechanical analyzer, load-cell data acquisition, calibration and uncertainty, ELISA, colorimetry, image processing.

Modelling

COMSOL and Abaqus multiphysics, needle mechanics, bioheat transfer, transport.

Automation

High-throughput workflows, simulation sweeps with built-in verification gates.

Eye physiology. Anatomy, barriers, thermal limits and mechanics of the eye decide what a device is allowed to do.

Projects

Anchoring microneedle depot for the eye

A drug-device combination product concept: a microneedle depot that holds itself in the outer wall of the eye and can be locked and unlocked on demand with mild heat, aimed at months-long antibody delivery without repeated injections.

ongoing (thesis)

Custom microneedle mechanical analyzer

A purpose-built tester that inserts, compresses and extracts microneedles while recording force and displacement, calibrated against dead weights, with an explicit error budget instead of a bare accuracy claim.

built, in commissioning

Microneedle geometry and mechanical stability

Finite-element studies of how needle shape sets insertion force, buckling and cutting performance, used to choose geometries before fabricating them.

presented (BMES 2025, ACS NORM 2024)

Ocular heating safety model

Multiphysics modelling of how heat applied at the eye's surface spreads through its layers, including the cooling effect of choroidal blood flow, to define what heating is safe.

ongoing

Design for additive manufacturing of microneedles

Predicting and compensating for the height lost when microneedles are printed by vat photopolymerization, so printed parts match their design.

submitted

Nanoparticle carriers in microneedle arrays

Nanoparticles loaded into microneedle arrays to carry antibodies and to extract interstitial fluid for liquid biopsy, from particle synthesis to core-shell needle design.

presented (IEEE NANO 2025, MSEC 2026)

High-throughput simulation sweeps with verification

A workflow that runs many simulations automatically, checks each one (conservation, independent solver, mesh convergence), and only lets verified results into the design maps.

in use
